Emerging Now

Topics whose rate of change is itself increasing, confirmed by at least two independent lines of evidence, and not merely large. Large established topics are separated out into Established & Mature — a topic can be popular and still be flat.

Before It Goes Viral

Probabilistic, not predictive. These are the strongest current candidates by burst onset and corroboration. There is no guarantee any of them will become mainstream, and this panel makes no accuracy claim.

Fastest Growing

Ranked by acceleration — the change in growth rate, which is what distinguishes "gaining" from "already popular".

Cross-Platform Signals

Topics independently appearing across two or more different data surfaces. Corroboration raises confidence; it does not create lead time.

Established & Mature

These are the big, well-known topics — and they are here precisely so you can see them excluded from the emerging list. A large topic with a flat rate of change is already established, and the system says so rather than ranking it as a discovery.

Manual Baseline

What a person browsing by hand would see right now — Reddit's own rising and top rankings, captured continuously instead of occasionally, plus Google's trending list. Compare it against Emerging Now: a topic that appears here before it reaches the manual surface is the system earning its keep.

Audience Questions

Questions and problems people are actually phrasing, taken verbatim from observed content and search suggestions, with the source link attached.

New Search Phrasings

Search and autocomplete phrasings observed for the first time in the last 72 hours. A phrasing that appears before there is content to answer it is one of the clearest early signals available for free.

Content Opportunities

Suggested angles derived strictly from what was observed — never invented, and screened so they do not imply unsupported medical claims.

Capture a Platform Trend List

TikTok and Instagram do not give automated collectors their trend data, and their terms prohibit scraping it. The legitimate route is for you to look at the page you are already logged in to, and paste the list here. The system timestamps it and starts tracking the topic from that moment — so a TikTok-originating topic gets the same baseline, acceleration and cross-source corroboration as everything else.

Captures are stored on Netlify Forms and picked up by the next collection cycle. For a fully offline path, run python -m collector.capture on your machine instead.

Plain English: what is actually being watched

The system reads publicly available, keyless pages — the kind any browser can load without logging in. It never signs in, never solves a CAPTCHA and never pretends to be a user.

  • Google Trends — the same "what is trending now" list you can see in a browser, for the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Spain, Finland and Sweden, plus the daily RSS feed of trending searches.
  • Google and YouTube autocomplete — the suggestions that appear as you type. Because these are generated from real query demand, a suggestion that has never appeared before is one of the earliest signs that people are starting to ask about something. The search terms are also rotated across English, Finnish and Spanish so each language builds its own history.
  • YouTube search — date-ordered results for each watched topic, read to count how many different creators are publishing about it. Supply-side spread is an early signal; one big channel is not the same as forty small ones.
  • Wikipedia pageviews — daily view counts for the article behind each concept. A concept whose article starts drawing traffic is often being noticed before it becomes a common search.
  • Google News RSS — coverage from the last 24 hours for each topic.
  • Research indexes — Europe PMC, PubMed and arXiv, which show not only what is being discussed but what is being studied, and how many papers a topic gained year over year.
  • Social surfaces — Reddit, Mastodon and Lemmy public feeds. Reddit's public RSS is being retired in November 2026, so that source is expected to fade.
  • Podcasts and Hacker News — new episodes and discussions matching each topic.

What it deliberately does not do: TikTok and Instagram expose no public trend API, so there is no TikTok or Instagram signal here — that is the platforms' position, not an oversight. Nothing on this site is inferred from engagement metrics the platform has not published.
